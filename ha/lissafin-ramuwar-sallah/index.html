Lissafin Ramuwar Sallah: Sallah Nawa Nake Bi?

Idan kun yi alama, za a ƙara bashin azumin Ramadan na wannan lokaci (kusan kwanaki 29–30 a shekara). Ku bar shi babu alama idan kun yi azumi.

Kwanakin Bashin da Aka Lissafa

Wannan kiyasi ne, ba hukunci ba. Yana lissafin kwanaki tsakanin kwanakin da ka shigar; rashin lafiya, tafiya da mantuwa ba a lissafa su ba. Idan kana shakka, tambayi masani.

Bibiyar ramuwar sallah

0Jimillar Sallolin da ake Bin ka

    Lissafa sallolin da ka rasa da azumin Ramadan daga ranar haihuwa, shekarun balaga a mazhabarka da ranar da ka fara sallah, sannan ka bibiye su.

    Tambayoyi da ake yawan yi

    A wane shekaru sallah take zama wajibi?

    Balaga ya dogara da alamu ba shekaru ba: mafi ƙanƙanta shekara 12 ga namiji da 9 ga mace; idan alamu ba su bayyana ba, ana ɗaukar mutum baligi idan ya cika shekara 15 (Hanafi). Idan ka san shekarun da alamar ta bayyana, zaɓi su. In ba haka ba, lissafi daga shekarun farko shi ne taka-tsantsan: ƙarin sallah ta zama nafila, amma farilla da aka rasa tana nan a matsayin bashi.

    Yaya ake ramawa sallolin da aka rasa a tafiya?

    Azahar, La'asar da Lisha da aka rasa a tafiya ana rama su raka'a 2.

    Wannan lissafi fatawa ne?

    Wannan kiyasi ne, ba hukunci ba. Yana lissafin kwanaki tsakanin kwanakin da ka shigar; rashin lafiya, tafiya da mantuwa ba a lissafa su ba. Idan kana shakka, tambayi masani.
