Hajj Guide: Step by Step, Day by Day, with Duas

The hajj day by day from 8 to 13 Dhu al-Hijjah: Mina, Arafah, Muzdalifah, stoning, sacrifice and tawaf, the kinds of hajj, duas and holy places.

The main text of this guide follows the Hanafi school; where the schools differ, the text says so. If your school is Shafi‘i, Maliki or Hanbali, the rulings that differ are also shown below (you can choose your school in the settings). For your own particular circumstances, consult the religious guide in your group or a scholar you trust.

Kinds of hajj

Hajj ifrad

Hajj performed by entering ihram for hajj alone.

Hajj tamattu‘

During the months of hajj (Shawwal, Dhu’l-Qa‘dah and the first ten days of Dhu’l-Hijjah), one first enters ihram for umrah, performs umrah and leaves ihram; then, in the same year, one enters ihram again in Makkah for hajj (usually on the Day of Tarwiyah).

Hajj qiran

One enters ihram for umrah and hajj together; after performing umrah one does not leave ihram, and the hajj is completed in the same ihram.

Fard · Wajib

Obligatory (fard), required (wajib) and sunnah acts

Fard: Ihram (condition) · Standing at Arafat (pillar) · Tawaf al-Ziyarah (al-Ifadah) (pillar)

Wajib: Standing at Muzdalifah · Sa‘y · Stoning the jamarat (ramy al-jimar) · Shaving or cutting the hair (halq or taqsir) · Sacrifice of thanksgiving in hajj tamattu‘ and qiran · On the day of Eid, keeping the order: first stoning, then (in tamattu‘ and qiran) the sacrifice, then shaving · For one who stands at Arafat during the day, remaining there until sunset · Performing tawaf al-ziyarah within the first three days of Eid · Farewell tawaf for those coming from outside Makkah (afaqi)

Duas

Talbiyah

On entering ihram and throughout ihram

لَبَّيْكَ اللّٰهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Reading: Labbayka Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, inna l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk, lā sharīka lak.

Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am! Here I am, You have no partner, here I am! Truly all praise and blessing are Yours, and all dominion; You have no partner.

Bukhari, Hajj 26; Muslim, Hajj 19

Intention for umrah

At the miqat, after the prayer of ihram

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ الْعُمْرَةَ فَيَسِّرْهَا لِي وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّي

Reading: Allāhumma innī urīdu l-‘umrata fa-yassirhā lī wa taqabbalhā minnī.

Meaning: O Allah! I intend to perform umrah; make it easy for me and accept it from me.

The wording of the intention found in the books of fiqh; the intention is made in the heart, and saying it aloud is good.

Intention for hajj

On entering ihram for hajj

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ الْحَجَّ فَيَسِّرْهُ لِي وَتَقَبَّلْهُ مِنِّي

Reading: Allāhumma innī urīdu l-ḥajja fa-yassirhu lī wa taqabbalhu minnī.

Meaning: O Allah! I intend to perform hajj; make it easy for me and accept it from me.

The wording of the intention found in the books of fiqh

  • On approaching Safa
  • On Safa and Marwah
  • At Arafat
  • When stoning the jamarat
  • When drinking Zamzam
  • Takbir al-tashriq
